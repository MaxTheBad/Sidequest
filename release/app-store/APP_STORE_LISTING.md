## Description

QuestHat helps you find people who want to make real plans.

Discover nearby quests based on your interests, browse them on a map, or create your own. Whether you want a workout partner, a book-club discussion, a creative session, or help staying accountable, QuestHat makes it easier to find people who are ready to show up.

With QuestHat you can:

- Discover local and virtual quests
- Browse plans in a visual feed or on a map
- Create a quest with a time, place, group size, and privacy controls
- Request to join approval-based quests
- Message hosts and other members
- Comment, save, share, and follow activity
- Control which notifications you receive
- Report objectionable content and block abusive users
- Temporarily deactivate or permanently delete your account

Exact meetup details can remain private until a host chooses to share them. QuestHat also includes reporting, blocking, content filtering, and a moderation process designed to keep the community useful and safe.

Stop leaving plans in the group chat. Find something real to do with QuestHat.

## Review Notes Draft

QuestHat is a social planning app with user-generated content, messaging, comments, location-based discovery, join requests, reporting, blocking, and account deletion.

The supplied review account should be entered directly in App Store Connect. Do not store its password in the repository or listing copy.

Suggested review path:

1. Log in with the review account.
2. Browse the home feed and switch between List and Map.
3. Open a quest to review media, comments, members, and join controls.
4. Open Create to review quest creation fields. Publishing a test quest is optional.
5. Open Settings > Preferences to review notification controls.
6. Open Settings > Blocked and use a listing's report flow to review safety controls.
7. Open Settings > Account to find temporary deactivation and permanent deletion.

No purchases, subscriptions, or paid features are included in this version.
